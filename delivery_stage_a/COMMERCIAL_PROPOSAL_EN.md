## 2. Opportunity & Discovery Engine

One engine, two modes:

- **Keyword mode** (the "best products around a search" you approved earlier): ranks the
  variants around a keyword by real demand, sell-through speed, competition and momentum.
- **Category / autonomous mode** (Phase 6): sweeps a **whole category with no keyword**, then
  surfaces the **rising** products you'd never have searched for — with **smart alerts** on the
  profitable ones (demand + fast turnover + low competition + upward momentum).

Both modes run on the **same detection engine**, so rather than charge the keyword engine (~$450)
and category discovery (~$500) separately, they're **bundled and credited** for the shared work.

**Price: $800 bundled** (keyword engine + autonomous discovery + alerts), instead of $950
charged separately. *Status: built.*

> Honest note on timing: the discovery/rising signal sharpens as history accumulates — it needs a
> few weeks of continuous collection (item 3) to reach full strength. The engine is built and runs
> now; it gets more accurate every day it collects.
